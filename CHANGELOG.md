# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (17 November 2026)

First public release.

- Site dashboard with NASA POWER monthly sun, temperature and wind, and the tools for each site's energy sources.
- Shading survey with camera skyline and sun-path diagram; panel tilt and azimuth; head survey for micro hydro; wind readings with a Weibull fit; meter readings.
- Over 30 calculators for solar PV, wind, micro hydro, geothermal (IAPWS-IF97 steam tables), storage, bioenergy, electrical and economics, each with two worked examples.
- Inspections (PV commissioning, small wind, micro hydro, battery room, work at height, LOTO) and daily report as PDF.
- Shared field tools: map and map library, GPS tracks, compass, sun compass, GPS camera.
- A guide for every screen and calculator behind the **?** button, and example data in Settings.
